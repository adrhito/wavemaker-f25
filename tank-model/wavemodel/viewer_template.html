A row moves water only when its bottom (its duct's ceiling) is under the still water. Air: the column trapped under the disc when the water rose past the bottom with the disc parked at 370 mm, then moved slowly here (tight seal). Rings: the frequency at which the air under the disc and the water in pipe and duct swing after a stroke. From the 2013-15 design drawings; the tank's posts and the motors' flanges are approximate.
