Presets
=======

These are starting points, not calibrated recipes. There is no
measured relationship yet between piston parameters and the wave
that results, so each one is named for what the PISTONS do rather
than for the wave it supposedly makes. Measure before you trust a
name.

Every value sits inside the limits in app/params.py, so the
application will accept all of them.

A row of zeroes for a piston means 'this piston was not part of
the preset'; it falls back to the All row when applied.

Gentle - short slow stroke
--------------------------

  A 120 mm stroke low in the travel, moving slowly.
  The smallest disturbance the array can make while still moving cleanly -- a starting point for calibration runs, and gentle on the drives.

Moderate - half stroke
----------------------

  A 250 mm stroke at middling speed.
  Roughly the machine's comfortable middle, and a sensible place to start when trying something new.

Full stroke - fast
------------------

  The whole travel, quickly.
  Close to the largest single-frequency disturbance the array can produce.

Maximum - everything at the limit
---------------------------------

  Every value at the limit the application allows.
  The hardest the machine is asked to work, and the case where pistons are most likely to fall behind their demanded position -- watch the array for stragglers.

Long period - slow with a pause
-------------------------------

  A long slow stroke with a dwell at each end and a sine profile, for the longest period the array can drive.
  Time 1 and Time 2 are the pause; if the units turn out not to be milliseconds, that is the number to adjust.

Sharp - hard acceleration, short stroke
---------------------------------------

  A short stroke driven as hard as possible, on a trapezoidal profile.
  Puts the energy into a brief sharp push rather than a long smooth one.

Travelling wave - front to back
-------------------------------

  Each column starts a little after the one in front of it, so the disturbance runs along the chamber instead of the whole array rising together.
  Curve Offset goes 0 at the front to 180 at the back.
  Run this with Start Curve; the stagger is what makes it a travelling wave rather than a standing one.

Travelling wave - fast march
----------------------------

  The same idea with a much smaller delay between columns, so the wave crosses the chamber quickly -- a shorter apparent wavelength than 'front to back'.

Focused - strongest in the middle
---------------------------------

  Full stroke through the middle of the chamber, tapering towards the front and back.
  Concentrates the disturbance rather than spreading it evenly.

Tapered - largest at the back
-----------------------------

  Stroke grows steadily from the front of the chamber to the back, so the array pushes progressively harder along its length.

Shallow water - front four columns
----------------------------------

  Values for the front four columns only, low in the travel, for use when the tank is shallow.
  Select the front four columns before applying it.
